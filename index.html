System 01 / 06

Growth
Mentorship™

Real-world guidance and accountability to grow strategically.

Most owners do not lack effort. They lack an outside operator who has seen the pattern before and will hold them to a plan. This is where everything starts: the assessment decides which systems you actually need, and in what order — so you never buy a tool you will not use.

  • Two to three free mentoring sessions
  • Full business health assessment
  • Personalized growth roadmap
  • Ongoing mentorship and accountability sessions (optional continuation)
  • Access to Stradigi’s systems and automation tools
System 02 / 06

Smart
Automation™

Save time and grow faster with AI-powered automation.

Every missed call, unanswered message and forgotten follow-up is a lead you already paid for. Automation answers, books, reminds and follows up while you are on a roof or with a patient — the routine moves without you, and you step in only for the conversations that need a person.

  • Chat & voice automation
  • Social media automation
  • Appointment scheduling with auto reminders
  • Lead follow-up sequences
  • Reputation automation (review requests + response management)
  • CRM integration
System 03 / 06

Authority
Builder™

Be the business everyone finds and trusts online.

When someone in Washington County searches for what you do, the business that shows up first with real reviews gets the call. Authority is built the slow, compounding way — listings that agree with each other, content that answers what people actually type, a review stream that keeps moving — and measured every month.

  • Local SEO optimization (keyword research, on-page, Google Business Profile)
  • Review & reputation management
  • Content marketing (blog + social aligned to target keywords)
  • Online listings & citations (NAP consistency)
  • Monthly performance analytics
System 04 / 06

Business
Engine™

Your all-in-one automation and CRM system.

A lead that lives in a text thread on your phone is not in a pipeline — it is a memory. The engine puts every conversation, stage and follow-up in one place, so nothing depends on who remembered what, and you can finally see where the money is stuck.

  • CRM & pipeline management
  • Automated sales workflows
  • Marketing integration
  • Customer communication hub (texts, calls, emails, chat in one place)
  • Analytics & reporting
System 05 / 06

Financial Clarity
System™

Know your numbers. Grow your business.

You cannot steer by a bank balance. Clean, closed books every month turn “I think we’re fine” into a statement you can read — what you earn, what you owe, what is coming — so hiring, pricing and spending become decisions instead of guesses.

  • QuickBooks Online management (setup, cleanup, maintenance)
  • Ramp credit card integration
  • Monthly book closing & reconciliation
  • Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow
  • Accounts payable management
  • Quarterly reviews & annual projections
  • Budgeting & ratio tracking
System 06 / 06

Productivity
Powerhouse™

Simplify your tech. Maximize your time.

The technology you run on should be invisible. When email, calendar, domains and your website are maintained by someone whose job it is, the hour you would lose to a plugin update or a locked account goes back to the work only you can do.

  • Google Workspace & Office365 management
  • GoDaddy domain management
  • WordPress CMS management (updates, backups, plugins)
  • Email & calendar integration
  • Tech support & troubleshooting
